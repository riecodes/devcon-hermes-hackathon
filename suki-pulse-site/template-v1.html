Every complaint at Suki Mart is a symptom. Suki Pulse lets Hermes find the operational cause behind it (a late rider, a supplier, a missing purchase order, a broken points ledger) and fix both sides.

How Hermes runs it: three connected layers

03 · THE FACE
Desktop plugin

A Suki Pulse pane in Hermes Desktop: pick a branch and click Pulse check, Fix top root cause or Recover riskiest customer. Results come back as native cards in the chat.

02 · THE PLAYBOOK
Skill suki-pulse

Triage, then root cause, then a plan covering both operations and customer recovery. It asks before it writes anything, then shows a before/after scorecard.

01 · THE HANDS
MCP server suki

10 domain tools. Read tools: triage, rollup, scorecard, customer story. Write tools: resolve, reply, reconcile points, draft POs, flag rider.

+ SYSTEM ONE
TypeSafe Jev

One call per complaint returns six yes/no root-cause checks, a churn score and urgency. It costs fractions of a cent and is cached. If Jev is unavailable, the tools fall back to keyword rules.

Why both tracks. The customer experience team sees angry tickets and the operations team sees late POs, but nobody joins them. Suki Pulse ranks a branch's complaints by cause, then shows the operational record behind each cause: riders' 30-day lateness across every branch they serve, perishables near expiry, items under reorder point with no purchase order, and points ledgers that don't add up.

Why Jev and Hermes. Jev is the fast classifier: it read all of the open complaints shown above in about a minute, for less than two cents. Hermes is the slow thinker: it reads the evidence, decides on the fixes, asks you, and then acts through the MCP tools.
